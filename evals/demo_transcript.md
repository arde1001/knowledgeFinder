# Demo: the same questions, six different people

Each person asks the domain expert the same questions. The expert only ever sees files that person may read. When the best match sits in a drive they cannot open, they are told which drive and whom to ask, and nothing more. Questions were chosen so that someone who reads everything answers them correctly and, where someone cannot read the file, so the notice appears; it appears for roughly three in four such questions, not all (see the benchmark files).
